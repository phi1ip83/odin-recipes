Cheddar and Chive Bake

Description

This copycat Starbucks potato, Cheddar, and chive bake uses frozen hash browns for quick and easy assembly at home. Cottage cheese adds a creamy, custardy texture and the bake has some added heat from hot sauce and Dijon mustard.

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 1/2 cups frozen diced hash browns
  • 1/2 cup chopped yellow onion
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt, divided
  • 1 cup packed fresh baby spinach, chopped
  • 12 large eggs
  • 1 1/3 cups 4% milkfat cottage cheese
  • 2 ounces Gruyère cheese, shredded
  • 8 ounces Cheddar cheese, shredded, divided
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh chives
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon hot sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Coat a 13x9-inch baking dish with cooking spray.
  2. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add hash browns, onion, and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until onions are soft and translucent and hash browns are tender, 6 to 7 minutes. Remove from heat; gently stir in spinach until combined and just wilted. Let cool for 5 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, process eggs, cottage cheese, Gruyère, and 1 cup Cheddar cheese in a blender until smooth, about 15 seconds. Stir in chives, Dijon mustard, hot sauce, pepper, and remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt until combined.
  4. Spread potato mixture evenly into the prepared baking dish. Pour egg mixture evenly over potato mixture. Sprinkle with remaining 1 cup Cheddar.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until center is set and top is lightly golden, 35 to 37 minutes. Let cool in baking dish for 10 minutes. Cut into 12 pieces and serve warm.
